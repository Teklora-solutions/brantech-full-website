How to Get & Use Your API Key

Your agent will guide you through this step, but you can also configure your key manually at any time.

1. Obtaining Your Key

Personal developer API keys (tkl_live_...) are issued by your Platform Administrator or Lead Engineer. Contact admin@teklora.co.ke or request one through internal staff channels. Administrators can issue and manage keys in the Django Admin →.

2. Where to Put Your Key

Store your key in your environment so your tools and MCP server can authenticate:

export TEKLORA_API_KEY="tkl_live_your_key"
